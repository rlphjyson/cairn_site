and is used under the [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/) (free
for commercial use, no attribution required; credit is given here anyway).
Each source was viewed and checked for legible brand logos and watermarks. The
files were cropped to squares (products) or resized (hero) and re-encoded as
WebP (350 and 700 px, hero 640-1600 px) with a JPEG fallback. `*-detail`
files are centre crops of the same photograph. Total image weight is about
1.5 MB.
